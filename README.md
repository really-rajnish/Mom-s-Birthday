# 🎂 Happy Birthday, Mom! ✨ Aesthetic Memory Scrapbook

A handcrafted, Pinterest-inspired digital memory scrapbook created with infinite love for Mom's birthday.

🔗 **Live Website**: [https://really-rajnish.github.io/Mom-s-Birthday/](https://really-rajnish.github.io/Mom-s-Birthday/)

---

## 🌸 Key Highlights & Architecture

### 1. 💌 The Vintage Envelope Cover
- Handcrafted cream stationery envelope with floating floral stamp, postal postmark, and a glowing wax seal.
- Smooth opening unfold animation with gentle acoustic chime and celebratory confetti eruption.

### 2. 🗂 The Confined 4×2 Square Cards Memory Board
- **Strictly confined single-screen viewport (`100vw × 100vh`) with zero page scrolling**.
- **8 Distinct Aesthetic Square Cards**:
  1. **Where My Story Began**: Vintage polaroid of Mom holding her baby girl with washi tape, perfectly framed so Mom's veil, head, and baby's dress are fully displayed.
  2. **Vintage Torn Quote**: Editorial newsprint snippet with torn deckled edges.
  3. **Walking in the Wind**: Deep brick-red Spotify vinyl card with interactive audio toggle.
  4. **Unpaid Therapist**: Kraft paper sticky note with tape and love pin.
  5. **You'll Always Be My Best-Tea**: Cute pastel doodle cups with blushing animations.
  6. **Interactive Birthday Cake**: Artisan bear cake with glowing candle you can tap to blow out with confetti!
  7. **You Get Me So High**: Minimalist white Spotify card honoring Mom as our lifelong best friend.
  8. **Milestone Family Polaroid**: Warm keepsake photo of Mom with her grown children at the temple steps.

### 3. 📖 8 Dedicated Immersive Story Pages
- Clicking any card opens its full, dedicated chapter page with rich typography, love letters, interactive candles, and memories.
- Complete navigation controls: "← Back to Board", Previous / Next buttons, and keyboard navigation (`Esc`, `←`, `→`).

---

## 🛠 100% Self-Contained & Zero Dependencies
- **No external asset dependencies**: All photos are embedded directly.
- **Synthesized Audio Engine**: Built with native Web Audio API oscillators that play a soft acoustic melody and chiming bells without needing external audio files.
- **Confetti & Particle Physics**: Built-in high-performance 2D canvas physics engine.

## 🚀 How to Enable GitHub Pages

1. Go to your repository on GitHub: **[really-rajnish/Mom-s-Birthday](https://github.com/really-rajnish/Mom-s-Birthday)**
2. Click **Settings** > **Pages** (under Code and automation in the left sidebar).
3. Under **Build and deployment > Source**:
   - Choose **GitHub Actions** (recommended — `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` will automatically build and deploy it!), OR
   - Choose **Deploy from a branch** > branch: `main` > folder: `/ (root)` > click **Save**.
4. Within 1-2 minutes, your website is live and shareable worldwide at:  
   👉 **[https://really-rajnish.github.io/Mom-s-Birthday/](https://really-rajnish.github.io/Mom-s-Birthday/)**
